Practice Classification

Select an innovator first, then review each practice's tags and 6M classification.

Select a practice to review tags, notes, and 6M classification.

Use only these 6M meanings:

  • Manpower: Trainings or capacity-building support
  • Method: Consulting, mentoring, technology transfer, processes, videos, SOPs, manuals, blogs
  • Market: Product/material purchase, market support, market reports
  • Material: Raw material supply
  • Machine: Machinery, plant setup
  • Money: Financial support
